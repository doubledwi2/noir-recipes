Noir D Mix

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Noir Mix (the "App"), a cocktail, mocktail, and drink recipe app published by Noir D Mix ("we", "us", "our"), handles information when you use it. We built this App to be as private as possible by design — most of what you do in the App never leaves your phone.

1. Information We Collect

Information you create in the App (stored only on your device):

The App does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal details to use it. When you mark a recipe as a favorite, or mark an ingredient as owned in "My Bar", that information is saved locally on your device only. It is never sent to us or to any server we operate — we simply don't have a server that stores this data. Uninstalling the App permanently deletes this local data.

Information collected automatically by third-party services we use:

  • Advertising (Google AdMob). The free version of the App shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device and advertising identifiers, approximate location, and app interaction data to show ads, including personalized ads where permitted by your device settings and region.
  • Subscriptions & purchases (Google Play Billing and RevenueCat). If you purchase a Pro subscription, the purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing — we never see or receive your payment card details. We use RevenueCat to check and manage your subscription status (e.g. confirming you're a Pro subscriber so the App can unlock full access). RevenueCat receives a purchase/transaction record and an anonymous subscriber identifier tied to your device, not your name or contact details.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information described above only to:

  • Show ads to free-tier users (via Google AdMob)
  • Verify and manage Pro subscription access (via Google Play Billing and RevenueCat)
  • Keep your favorites and "My Bar" selections available between app sessions, on your own device

We do not sell your information, and we do not have a database of user accounts to sell from in the first place.

3. Third-Party Services

The App relies on the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy, which we encourage you to review:

  • Google AdMob / Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing
  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Since favorites and "My Bar" data live only on your device, deleting or uninstalling the App removes them completely — there is nothing for us to delete on our end, because we never received that data. Purchase and subscription records are retained by Google Play and RevenueCat according to their own retention policies; you can request access or deletion of that data directly through Google Play's account tools or by contacting us at the email below, and we'll pass the request to RevenueCat on your behalf.

5. Children's Privacy

Noir Mix includes recipes that reference alcoholic beverages and is intended for adults of legal drinking age in their country. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us and we will address it.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information held about you (for example, under GDPR in the EU/EEA or CCPA in California). Because the App itself stores your usage data only on your own device, most such requests concern the third-party services above. Contact us and we'll help direct your request.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when we add a new feature that changes what data is collected. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the latest revision. We encourage you to review it occasionally.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, contact us at noirdmix@gmail.com.
